# Campus Insider Brand Guide

This is the canonical brand and messaging guidelines document for **Campus Insider**. It serves as the source of truth for all marketing, copywriters, designers, and AI agents.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

* **What it is**: Campus Insider is an independent, founder-led decision-support platform designed to help international students choose the right university and degree program in Turkey.
* **The Problem it Solves**: Traditional education agencies act as sales brokers. They push students toward expensive universities to maximize commissions, hiding hidden costs (like prep school fees) and omitting the realities of student life in Turkey.
* **Why it Exists**: To bring trust, transparency, and honesty to the international student journey in Turkey.
* **What Makes it Different**: We are **not** an agency. We do not sell; we analyze, filter, and match based on strict student-provided budget and academic criteria.
* **Desired Customer Perception**: A trusted older brother who has already navigated the exact path the student is starting.
* **Brand Promise**: *"We will recommend the option we would choose ourselves if we were spending our own money."*

---

## 2. Positioning

* **Independent Support over Affiliate Brokerage**: Campus Insider is positioned strictly as a **decision-support platform**, not an education broker.
* **Objective Advisory**: The objective is not to convince everyone to study in Turkey. The objective is to help students decide *whether* Turkey is the right choice for them. If yes, we help them select the best fit.
* **Founder-Led Authenticity**: Built on the direct experience of its founder, Abdullah. It is an honest, personal consultation scaled by technology.

---

## 3. Founder Story & Key Copy

The core message of the brand is: **"Let me help you avoid the mistakes I made."**

### Exact Approved Copy
Use these exact quotes in hero copy, personal highlights, and CTAs:
* *"I'm not here to convince you to study in Türkiye. I'm here to help you decide whether it's the right choice for you."*
* *"I'll help you make the same decision I'd make if I were spending my own money."*
* *"Let me help you avoid the mistakes I made."*

### Messaging Principles
* When writing on behalf of Abdullah, speak in the **first person** (`I`, `me`, `my`).
* Do not speak in corporate plural (`we`, `our company`, `our agency`) when discussing advisory.
* Emphasize lessons learned the hard way (hidden costs, bad accommodation, wrong language tracks).

---

## 4. Brand Personality

### Reassuring
* **This means**: Calmly outlining options, being clear about tuition fees, and validating student concerns.
* **This does NOT mean**: Over-promising immediate visas or guaranteeing 100% acceptance.

### Youthful & Modern
* **This means**: Clean, editorial layouts, fresh copy, and digital-first responsiveness.
* **This does NOT mean**: Using excessive slang or looking like a generic crypto/fintech SaaS.

### Transparent
* **This means**: Listing cash vs installment prices and prep school costs upfront.
* **This does NOT mean**: Hiding fees or obfuscating currency conversions.

### Human
* **This means**: Sounding like a real mentor or a senior student.
* **This does NOT mean**: Sounding like a legal department or a corporate brochure.

### Independent-Minded
* **This means**: Giving objective assessments, even if it means pointing out when a university does not fit a student.
* **This does NOT mean**: Blindly promoting universities with the highest referral commission.

---

## 5. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Tone Map by Channel
* **Website**: Clear, editorial, structured, value-first.
* **Paid Ads**: Problem-focused (e.g., "avoid university scams"), guiding to the free assessment tool.
* **WhatsApp**: Direct, helpful, friendly, and non-intrusive.
* **Audience (Students)**: Empowering, sibling-like guidance.
* **Audience (Parents)**: Safe, financially responsible, clear about accreditations.

### Writing Rules
* **Words to Favor**: *Blueprint, Map, Waypoints, Match, Options, Budget, Clear, Verify, Realistic.*
* **Words to Avoid**: *Guaranteed, Official Partner, 100% Free Scholarship, Secret Discount, Urgent, Apply Now!*
* **Sentence Style**: Short, punchy, active voice.
* **Humor**: Subtle, warm, never sarcastic.
* **Urgency**: Avoid false timers or fake scarcity. Emphasize natural deadlines (e.g., "autumn registration closes in 3 weeks").
* **Emojis**: Use selectively for visual highlights (e.g., 📍, 🎓, 💳, 💡, 💬). Never use strings of multiple emojis.

5. **Conversion CTAs**:
   * *"Find My Options"* (Main Funnel Entry).

## 7. Claims & Trust Rules (Verification Guardrails)

To maintain absolute trust, marketing copy must **NEVER** claim:
* 🛑 **Guaranteed Admission**: Admission is subject to final university verification.
* 🛑 **Guaranteed Scholarships**: Only state achievements or official tuition discounts listed in the database.
* 🛑 **Unverified Rankings**: Use terms like "established private university" instead of "top 10".
* 🛑 **Fake Scarcity**: Never show fake countdown timers or fake slots remaining.

### Safe Alternatives:
* *Instead of*: "Get 70% Scholarship!" → *Use*: "Get the official cash prepayment discount."
* *Instead of*: "We guarantee you get in!" → *Use*: "Verify if you meet the entry criteria in 5 minutes."

## 8. Visual Identity (The "Istanbul Student Atlas" V3)

Prioritize the newer editorial typography and color system over legacy SaaS visual guidelines.

### Color Palette
* **Ink Navy** (`#152238`): Dominant text color and headers.
* **Cobalt Blue** (`#3157D5`): Primary accents, waypoint labels, and core buttons.
* **Warm Sand** (`#F3EEE5` / `#F9F6F0`): Warm, editorial background color.
* **Terracotta** (`#D96C4A`): Accent highlights for quote tags or alternatives.
* **Sea Green** (`#168B83`): Used for secondary success components and budget items.

### Typography
* **Headings**: Sora (Bold, clean geometric sans).
* **Body Text**: Inter or Manrope (Highly readable, neutral).
* **Quotes**: Source Serif 4 or Playfair Display (Serif, editorial).

---

## 9. Photography Direction

* **YES**: Authentic candid campus life, real Istanbul landscapes (e.g., Galata waterfront), and real images of founder Abdullah.
* **NO**: Staged corporate handshakes, generic graduation cap tossing stock models, and overly polished AI-generated human faces.

---

## 10. Logo & University Identity

* **Aspect Ratio**: Always preserve the aspect ratio of university logos. Never stretch or squeeze them.
* **Size**: Render logos inside a uniform `72x72` transparent container.
* **Fallback**: When an official logo is missing from the local library (`public/universities/`), render a clean placeholder seal using the university's initials.

---

## 11. CTA System & Funnel

1. **Top of Funnel**: Allow users to explore options without signups using `"Find My Options"`.
2. **Contact Stage**: Do not push user details collection too early. Lead with value.
3. **WhatsApp Usage**: Introduce WhatsApp redirect buttons on recommendation cards *after* the results have been processed, framing it as: `"Ask Abdullah on WhatsApp"`.

---

## 12. Audience Communication

* **Early-Stage Researchers**: Focus on comparative programs and cost differences.
* **High-Intent Applicants**: Focus on visa timelines, application details, and deposit structures.
* **Parents**: Emphasize safety, recognized accreditations, and realistic tuition fees.

---

## 13. Good vs. Bad Copy Examples

* ❌ **Bad (Salesy)**: *"🔥 GET 70% SCHOLARSHIP IN TURKEY NOW! LIMITED SEATS!!!"*
*   **Good (Campus Insider)**: *"See which universities fit your budget before you apply."*
* ❌ **Bad (Vague)**: *"The official portal for all Turkish universities."*
*   **Good (Campus Insider)**: *"An independent tool to map and verify programs in Istanbul."*

---

## 14. Brand Non-Negotiables

1. **Be Honest**: Tell students about prep school fees and living costs upfront.
2. **Never Sell**: Educate the user; do not pitch them.
3. **No Hotlinks**: Serve all university logo assets locally.
4. **Use First-Person for Advisory**: Let Abdullah's voice guide the counseling.
5. **No AI Humans**: Avoid fake generated stock students.
6. **No Fake Scarcity**: Never use countdown clocks or fake seat limits.
7. **No Guaranteed Banners**: Never advertise "100% Guaranteed Admission".
8. **Keep Layouts Asymmetric & Editorial**: Build using clean vertical grids, large text, and sand/navy aesthetics.
9. **Zero-Match Grace**: If a student matches 0 programs, offer custom manual help instead of empty errors.
10. **Protect Student Trust**: Prioritize precision over clickbait copy.
